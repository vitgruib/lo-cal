Subject: Goggles events + Lo-Cal

Hi OCTO team,

I'm Ethan, a Berkeley student, and I'm reaching out because I've been building something that overlaps with what you're doing with Berkeley Goggles.

Lo-Cal (https://vitgruib.github.io/lo-cal/) is an open-source calendar that pulls about 2,000 upcoming Berkeley events into one place, from Luma, the campus calendar, dozens of club and department calendars, Partiful, hackathon sites, local venues, Instagram, mailing lists, and Slack. It rebuilds every 3 hours. You can filter by day, topic, area, or free food, subscribe to a topic in Google Calendar, and share any event with its own link.

The pain point I'm tackling is that there is no unified source for events, and students must look through various sources to keep up on all the events everywhere. Club events are the biggest gap: most of them live behind CalLink's login or on Instagram, so even students who want to find them often can't.

I work with transparency as my key tenet. All the code is open source, the About page lays out every source and every tagging rule, and the events themselves are public: other services can already pull them automatically as JSON or calendar feeds. It's also interfaced so personal LLMs can easily look through the site, so anyone can ask their own ChatGPT or Claude what's happening tonight and get real listings.

That's why I'm writing. Goggles already has the best picture of Berkeley's clubs, and Lo-Cal is built around events, so the two seem to fit together well. Could Goggles share its events as a feed (ICS or JSON) so they show up on Lo-Cal? Goggles is welcome to use Lo-Cal's events in return, as a feed or in whatever form is most useful to you. More broadly, I'd love to hear how OCTO thinks about tools like this and whether there's a way to work together.

Happy to meet if that's easier. Thanks for reading!

Ethan
